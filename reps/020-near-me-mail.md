## Summary

EDDM co-op postcard business targeting Denver home service businesses via Facebook groups. Oversized 9x12 postcards filled with local ads (one business per category), distributed to 10,000+ homes via USPS Every Door Direct Mail. Upsell path to AI-powered local marketing services (web, Google Ads, SEO).

## The Model

You fill a 9x12 postcard with ads from local home service businesses — one plumber, one landscaper, one roofer, one HVAC company, etc. No competing categories on the same card. Then you use USPS Every Door Direct Mail (EDDM) to send that card to thousands of targeted homeowners in a specific zip code — no mailing list needed, just pick the zip codes and the post office handles delivery at 25 cents per home.

The sales motion: post in local business networking Facebook groups (not neighborhood groups — business groups, because members are already in marketing mode). Short post, "first come first served" urgency, respond via Messenger. Josh (the case study) closed his first deal — $1,100 from a plumber — over 5 text messages.

**Unit economics per 10,000-home mailing:**
- Postage: $2,500
- Printing (14pt UV gloss): $2,300
- Total cost: $4,800
- Revenue (15 spots × $600): $9,000
- Gross profit: ~$4,200
- Gross margin: ~47%

## Entry Assessment

**Market reality: ✅** Local home service businesses are active buyers of local marketing. Denver has a dense home services market. EDDM is a live, verified USPS product.

**Wedge:** Exclusivity per category + free ad design + QR code tracking. A landscaper only needs one new client to recoup a $600 ad spend. The pitch sells itself.

**Competition:** Valpak, Money Mailer, and co-op mail franchises exist but operate at scale with franchise fees and cold-call infrastructure. The solo EDDM + Facebook approach is a genuinely open lane — no established player uses this distribution model.

**Lift to first revenue:** Post in Facebook groups before spending a cent. Fill the card before paying the printer. Capital requirement is near zero if executed correctly.

## Execution Notes

The bottleneck is card-filling speed. Need 15-16 local businesses at $600 each per mailing. That's the job — and it's a sales job done entirely via Facebook DMs.

**Repeat business:** Go back to every advertiser before opening new spots for the next card. Warm relationship fills the next card dramatically faster than cold outreach.

**AI leverage:**
- Claude writes the Facebook group posts and DM scripts
- Canva + AI handles ad design
- QR code tracking is built into Canva/Adobe Express
- Upsell path: every advertiser is a warm lead for website builds, Google Ads management, local SEO — all deliverable with AI assistance

## The Upsell Play

The real ceiling isn't the postcard — it's the local marketing services business the postcard creates. Every business owner who buys a $600 spot has already proven they'll spend money on local marketing. Upsell to:
- Website builds ($500–2,000, buildable with Claude in hours)
- Google Ads management ($300–500/month)
- Local SEO ($200–400/month)

You're getting paid to generate your own client base for a local AI-powered marketing operation.

## Exit Mode

**Sub-4-hour target.** Design and scheduling delegatable to VA. Sales motion is templated (same post, same pitch, same follow-up). Systematizable within 2-3 cards.
