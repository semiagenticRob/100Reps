# Rep 022 — No Knock

**Status:** Idea
**Repo:** [semiagenticRob/no-knock](https://github.com/semiagenticRob/no-knock)

Community enforcement layer for no-soliciting ordinances. Address registry, QR-enabled signs, one-tap violation reporting, and city dashboard. Arvada pilot with scale-to-municipalities path.
