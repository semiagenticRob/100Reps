# Rep 026 — Monetizing the Megawatt

**Status:** Live
**Repo:** [semiagenticRob/monetizing-the-megawatt](https://github.com/semiagenticRob/monetizing-the-megawatt)

Interactive visualizer for a Bitcoin mining economics paper published at Bitcoin Park. Implements the paper's parametric model (Hashrate + Heat Credits + Load Credits − Electricity − Opex = Profit) as an interactive Sankey diagram with adjustable sliders and real-world case study presets.
