# Rep 008 — Declutter Truck

**Status:** Idea | **Tier:** Idea | **Type:** Physical ops + marketplace

Mobile truck that shows up at your house like a donation pickup, but you pay a flat fee for removal. AI-assisted photo cataloging at pickup generates an itemized receipt. Customer agrees on-site what gets donated vs. listed for sale.

## Key Insight

The in-person consent moment at the truck is the trust layer — not a form, a conversation.
