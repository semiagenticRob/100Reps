[[rep:13]] Eyes on Arvada launched today — posted to Reddit, published the newsletter, reached out to city council members directly. For the first time across all the reps, there was immediate, genuine signal that people cared. Not just pageviews or politeness — actual interest.

What's notable is that the *why* isn't fully clear yet. It works, but there's no clean explanation for why this one landed when others didn't. That ambiguity is worth sitting with rather than rushing to explain away. The instinct is to reverse-engineer a formula, but the more honest read is: follow the signal first, understand it later.

The rep is also different in character from everything else. Most reps are about reach and scale — get it in front of as many people as possible. This one is the opposite. It's hyper-local, deliberately small, and requires actual human engagement with stakeholders in a new domain. That friction turned out to be a feature. Getting out and talking to people in a specific community produced a response that broadcasting to a general audience never did.

There's something potentially fat-tailed about the hyperlocal + AI angle that hasn't been fully explored. The same pipeline could work for any municipality. But scaling it thoughtfully — maybe city by city, each with a local operator — is a very different model than most of the reps. That's worth a dedicated strategy session before assuming it should scale the same way everything else does.

Monetization is genuinely unclear right now, and that's okay. The instinct to solve it immediately is a trap — the right move is to grow the audience first and let the monetization model emerge from what the audience actually values. Advertising, sponsorships, a paid tier for civic organizations, a licensing model for other cities — all of these are on the table but none should be forced yet.

A meeting with the Arvada mayor is scheduled for next week. That's a new kind of stakeholder relationship — one that doesn't fit the usual founder-to-customer pattern. Worth being thoughtful about how to show up in that conversation.
