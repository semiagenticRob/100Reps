Milestone: 20 reps. And with it, the first real sense of rep fatigue — not from volume, but from a pattern emerging in how reps stall.

## The Holding-Pattern Problem

Reps seem to hit a plateau state where progress requires one of two things:

1. **Product execution** — building new features, improving the core experience. [[rep:002]] is the example here: it's live, but the next level requires shipping more functionality. That work falls on the human.

2. **Active selling** — going out and closing. [[rep:014]] is the example: the funnel exists, the assets are ready, but someone has to actually send the messages and get on calls. That also falls on the human.

Both bottlenecks currently require human execution, and at 20 reps, those accumulate faster than they clear.

[[rep:018]] is the outlier — it's in a stable holding state that doesn't demand either. It just exists and works. That's a different kind of rep.

## The Question

Is there a useful new categorization for reps in this plateau state? Something beyond `live` that signals *what kind of human action is blocking the next level* — execution vs. selling? And is there an AI leverage point that could absorb one or both of those gaps over time?

No answers yet. Just noticing the shape of the problem at 20 reps.
