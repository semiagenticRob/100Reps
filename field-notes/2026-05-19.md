Spent time across several non-technical industries this week — politics, brick-and-mortar retail, trades (plumbing, electrical). The pattern is striking: the businesses starting to integrate AI are seeing immediate gains, while the ones who haven't touched it yet aren't registering the threat. They see the power in an abstract sense, but they haven't internalized that this is an existential, log-order change — not just a productivity upgrade.

We haven't hit the inflection point yet. The tide is going out, but most people aren't on the beach watching it. The window where early movers have a meaningful advantage over the holdouts is still wide open — and that's exactly where the training and onboarding work sits.

The interesting thing isn't who's getting it. It's who *isn't* getting it yet, and how fast that changes once one player in a given industry moves hard.
